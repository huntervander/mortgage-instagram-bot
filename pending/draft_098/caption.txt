What credit score do you actually need to buy a home in Florida? 🏠

Here's a quick breakdown by loan type — and you might be surprised how accessible homeownership can be:

🏦 **Conventional Loan** — Typically 620+ minimum. A score of 740+ unlocks better interest rates and lower (or no) PMI costs.

🏡 **FHA Loan** — As low as 580 with 3.5% down, or 500–579 with 10% down (lender-dependent). A great option for first-time buyers working on their credit.

🎖️ **VA Loan** — No official minimum set by the VA, though most lenders look for 620+. One of the most flexible loan programs available — a well-earned benefit for those who've served.

🌾 **USDA Loan** — Generally 640+ for streamlined approval. Available for eligible rural and some suburban Florida areas.

The bottom line: your credit score is just one piece of the puzzle. Lenders also look at income, debt-to-income ratio, and employment history — so even if your score isn't perfect, there may be a path forward.
