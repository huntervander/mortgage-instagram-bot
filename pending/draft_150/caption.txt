FHA vs. Conventional: Which loan is right for you? 🏡

If you're buying your first home in Florida, you've probably seen both terms — but what's the real difference?

Here's a quick breakdown:

🔵 FHA Loan
• Down payment as low as 3.5% (with a 580+ credit score)
• More flexible qualifying guidelines
• Gift funds allowed for your down payment
• Requires mortgage insurance (MIP) — upfront + monthly

⚪ Conventional Loan
• Down payment starting at 3% (for qualified buyers)
• No upfront mortgage insurance
• PMI can be removed once you reach 20% equity
• Generally better rates for buyers with 700+ credit scores

Neither is automatically "better" — the right fit depends on your credit score, down payment savings, and long-term goals.
