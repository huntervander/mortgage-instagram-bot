Did you know your credit score plays a major role in the mortgage rate you receive? 🏠

Understanding your credit profile before you apply can make a big difference in your homebuying journey. Here's a quick overview of how lenders generally view credit tiers:

• 760+ — Typically qualifies for the most competitive rates available
• 700–759 — Strong position with excellent loan options
• 620–699 — Still eligible for programs like FHA and VA loans
• Below 620 — There are still paths forward — let's talk!

A few ways to strengthen your score before you apply:
✅ Pay down high-balance credit cards
✅ Avoid opening new lines of credit before closing
✅ Review your credit report and dispute any errors
✅ Keep on-time payments consistent — payment history is the biggest factor

Even a small improvement in your score can potentially lower your rate and save you money over the life of your loan. The best time to start is now, even if you're months away from buying.
