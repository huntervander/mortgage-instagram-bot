APR vs. Interest Rate — do you know the difference? 🏠

When you're shopping for a home loan in Florida, you'll see two numbers: the interest rate and the APR (Annual Percentage Rate). They're both important — and knowing the difference could save you thousands.

Here's the breakdown:

📌 Interest Rate — The base cost of borrowing the money, expressed as a percentage. This is what determines your monthly principal + interest payment.

📌 APR — The bigger-picture number. It includes the interest rate PLUS lender fees (like origination fees and discount points). Because it captures the true cost of the loan, APR is always equal to or higher than the interest rate.

💡 Pro tip: When comparing loan offers side by side, compare APRs — not just interest rates. A loan with a lower rate but higher fees could cost you more over the life of the loan.
