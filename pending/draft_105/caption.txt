Fixed-Rate vs. Adjustable-Rate Mortgage — which one is right for you? 🏡

Choosing the right loan type can save you thousands over the life of your mortgage. Here's a quick breakdown:

🔒 Fixed-Rate Mortgage
Your interest rate stays the same for the entire loan term (15 or 30 years). Your principal + interest payment never changes — so you always know what to budget for. Great if you plan to stay in your home long-term and want stability.

📈📉 Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM)
An ARM starts with a lower fixed rate for an initial period (commonly 5, 7, or 10 years), then adjusts periodically based on a market index. The lower starting rate can mean savings early on — but there's uncertainty once the adjustment period begins.

So which should you choose?
• Planning to stay 10+ years? A fixed rate often wins for peace of mind.
• Selling or refinancing before the adjustment kicks in? An ARM could work in your favor.
• Ask about your break-even point — that's the key number!
