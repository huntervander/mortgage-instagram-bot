FHA Loans: Your Path to Homeownership Starts Here 🏡

Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again. FHA loans are designed to make homeownership more accessible — here's what you need to know:

✅ Down payment as low as 3.5% (with a 580+ credit score)
✅ More flexible credit requirements than conventional loans
✅ Competitive interest rates
✅ Gift funds from family can be used toward your down payment

FHA loans are especially popular with first-time buyers across Florida, and they can be paired with state assistance programs like Florida Housing's down payment help — potentially lowering your upfront costs even further.

If you've been sitting on the sidelines waiting to "save up enough," an FHA loan might be the key that opens your front door sooner than you think.
