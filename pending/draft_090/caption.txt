Your credit score could save — or cost — you thousands on your mortgage. 👀

Here's what every homebuyer should know:

💳 Score ranges matter more than you think
Even a 20-40 point difference in your credit score can move your interest rate enough to change your monthly payment by hundreds of dollars over the life of a 30-year loan. Lenders use your score to assess risk — the higher your score, the better the rate you qualify for.

📊 The 5 factors that shape your score:
• Payment history (~35%) — the biggest factor, bar none. Pay on time, every time.
• Credit utilization (~30%) — keep balances below 30% of your credit limit
• Length of credit history (~15%) — older accounts help, so think twice before closing them
• Credit mix (~10%) — a healthy mix of revolving and installment credit is a plus
• New inquiries (~10%) — avoid opening new lines of credit right before applying for a mortgage

✅ Quick wins before you apply:
- Pay down revolving balances where you can
- Check your credit report for errors — you're entitled to free reports at AnnualCreditReport.com
- Hold off on any large new purchases on credit until after closing
