Did you know you could buy a home with as little as 3.5% down? 🏠

FHA loans are one of the most popular options for first-time homebuyers — and for good reason. Here's what makes them stand out:

✅ Low down payment — as little as 3.5% (with a qualifying credit score)
✅ More flexible credit requirements than conventional loans
✅ Down payment can come from a family gift or eligible assistance program
✅ Available through many Florida Housing programs for added support

An FHA loan is backed by the Federal Housing Administration, which allows lenders to offer more flexible terms. This can be a great fit if you're earlier in your homebuying journey or still building your credit history.
