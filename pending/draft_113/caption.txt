Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again. 🏡

This is one of the biggest myths holding first-time buyers back — and the reality is much more accessible than most people realize.

Here are the real minimum down payments by loan type:

✅ FHA Loan — as low as 3.5% down (580+ credit score)
✅ Conventional Loan — as low as 3% down for qualified buyers
✅ USDA Loan — 0% down in eligible rural Florida areas
✅ VA Loan — 0% down for eligible veterans & active-duty service members

Florida homebuyers also have access to programs like the Florida Hometown Heroes Program and Florida Housing Finance Corporation assistance — which can help cover your down payment AND closing costs.
