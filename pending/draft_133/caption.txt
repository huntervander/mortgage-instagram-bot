FHA or Conventional — which loan is right for you? 🏠

It's one of the most common questions we hear from first-time buyers, and the answer depends on your specific situation. Here's a quick breakdown:

🔵 FHA Loans:
• Minimum 3.5% down (with 580+ credit score)
• More flexible credit requirements
• Requires mortgage insurance premium (MIP) — in most cases for the life of the loan

🟡 Conventional Loans:
• As low as 3% down for qualified buyers
• Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) can be removed once you reach 20% equity
• Typically requires a stronger credit profile (620+)

The right choice comes down to your credit score, savings, and long-term goals. A quick conversation with a loan officer can help you figure out which path saves you the most money over time.
