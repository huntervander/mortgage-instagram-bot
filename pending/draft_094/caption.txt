FHA vs. Conventional Loans — What's the Difference? 🏠

If you're buying a home in Florida for the first time, two loan types you'll hear a lot about are FHA and Conventional. Here's a quick breakdown:

🔵 FHA Loans:
• Minimum 3.5% down payment (with a 580+ credit score)
• More flexible with past credit challenges
• Requires mortgage insurance (MIP) — stays for the life of the loan if you put less than 10% down

⚪ Conventional Loans:
• Down payments as low as 3% (though 5–20% is common)
• Generally requires a 620+ credit score
• Private mortgage insurance (PMI) automatically cancels once you reach 20% equity

Neither option is automatically "better" — the right fit depends on your credit profile, savings, and long-term goals. That's exactly what we help you figure out.
