Did you know you might not need 20% down to buy a home? 🏠

Many homebuyers are shocked to learn that a full 20% down payment is NOT required for most loan programs. Here's a quick breakdown:

• FHA Loans — as low as 3.5% down with a qualifying credit score
• Conventional Loans — some programs start at just 3% down for eligible buyers
• USDA Loans — 0% down for eligible rural and suburban Florida properties
• VA Loans — 0% down for qualifying veterans and active-duty service members

Florida residents may also qualify for the Florida Hometown Heroes program, which offers down payment and closing cost assistance for eligible first-time buyers.

The "20% down" rule is a myth for most buyers — and waiting to save that much could cost you more in the long run as home prices change.
