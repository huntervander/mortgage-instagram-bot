Florida has a program that helps teachers, nurses, firefighters, and more buy a home — and most people have never heard of it 🏡

Meet the **Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program** — a down payment and closing cost assistance program for full-time Florida workers who serve their communities every day.

**Who qualifies?**
✅ Teachers, nurses, EMTs, firefighters, law enforcement
✅ Active military and veterans
✅ Many other community workforce occupations
✅ Must be a Florida resident and work full-time in FL
✅ First-time homebuyer (or not owned a primary home in the last 3 years)

**What does it offer?**
The program can provide significant help with your down payment and closing costs — making homeownership more accessible even if you haven't been able to save a large lump sum.

Combined with the right loan type (Conventional, FHA, VA, or USDA), it can dramatically reduce the cash you need to bring to closing.

**The fine print:** Program funds are limited and availability can change. The best way to find out if you qualify is to get pre-qualified with a participating lender.
