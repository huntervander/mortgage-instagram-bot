Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Here's what most people don't know. 🏡

The down payment myth stops a lot of would-be homeowners before they even start. The truth? You have more options than you think:

• **Conventional loans** — as low as 3% down for qualified buyers
• **FHA loans** — just 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score
• **VA loans** — 0% down for eligible veterans and active-duty military (zero PMI too!)
• **USDA loans** — 0% down in eligible rural and suburban Florida areas
• **Florida Hometown Heroes** — down payment AND closing cost assistance for community workers buying their primary home in Florida

The right program depends on your credit, income, and the home you're buying. There's no one-size-fits-all — which is exactly why talking to a licensed loan officer matters.
