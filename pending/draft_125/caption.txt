Think you need 20% down to buy a home? Think again. 🏠

FHA loans are one of the most popular options for first-time buyers in Florida — and for good reason:

✅ Down payment as low as 3.5% (with a 580+ credit score)
✅ More flexible credit and income requirements than conventional loans
✅ Gift funds can be applied toward your down payment
✅ Available for purchasing or refinancing a primary residence

One thing to keep in mind: FHA loans require mortgage insurance premiums (MIP) — both upfront and monthly. This protects the lender, but it does add to your monthly payment. It's worth comparing FHA vs. Conventional to see which fits your financial goals best.
