Pre-Qualified vs. Pre-Approved — Do You Know the Difference? 🏠

Many homebuyers use these terms interchangeably, but they're not the same — and the difference could impact your offer in a big way.

Pre-Qualification is a quick, informal snapshot. You share basic info (income, debts, assets) and a lender gives you a rough estimate of what you may qualify for. No documentation required. Great for early planning!

Pre-Approval is the real deal. A lender reviews your actual documents — pay stubs, tax returns, credit report — and issues a conditional commitment letter. Sellers take this seriously, especially in Florida's competitive market.

Bottom line: if you're ready to make offers, get pre-approved first. It strengthens your position, removes uncertainty, and shows sellers you mean business.
