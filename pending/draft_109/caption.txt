🏠 Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again!

One of the biggest myths in homebuying is that a large down payment is required. Here's what your options actually look like:

✅ FHA Loan — as little as 3.5% down (with qualifying credit score)
✅ Conventional Loan — some programs start at just 3% down
✅ USDA Loan — 0% down for eligible rural and suburban areas
✅ VA Loan — 0% down for eligible veterans and active-duty service members

And if you're a Florida resident? The Florida Hometown Heroes program offers down payment and closing cost assistance to help qualifying buyers bridge the gap.

The right loan for you depends on your credit, income, and the home you're buying — which is exactly what we help you sort out.
