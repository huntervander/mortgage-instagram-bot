🏡 What Credit Score Do You Actually Need to Buy a Home?

One of the most common questions we get: "Is my credit score good enough to qualify?" The answer may surprise you — you don't need a perfect score to become a homeowner.

Here's a quick breakdown by loan type:
• Conventional: typically 620+ (best rates at 740+)
• FHA: as low as 580 with 3.5% down
• VA loan: no government minimum — lenders generally look for 580–620+
• USDA: typically 640+

Not there yet? Small steps can move the needle faster than you think:
✅ Pay down credit card balances (keep utilization under 30%)
✅ Avoid opening new accounts before applying
✅ Dispute any errors on your credit report — they're more common than you'd think

Your credit score is a starting point, not the finish line. 💪
