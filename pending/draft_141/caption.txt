Did you know Florida has programs to help first-time homebuyers get into their dream home? 🏡

Here are 3 programs worth knowing about:

📌 Florida Hometown Heroes — Designed for community workers like teachers, nurses, firefighters, and law enforcement. Offers down payment and closing cost assistance up to 5% of the loan amount for eligible borrowers.

📌 Florida Housing Finance Corporation — Provides statewide down payment assistance and bond programs for eligible first-time buyers. Income and purchase price limits apply, so it's worth checking your eligibility.

📌 FHA Loans — With as little as 3.5% down, FHA loans remain one of the most accessible options for buyers with credit scores starting at 580. A great starting point if you're just getting started.

Every buyer's situation is different — and knowing which program fits you could save you thousands upfront.
