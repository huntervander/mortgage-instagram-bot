Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again. 🏡

One of the biggest myths in homebuying is that you need a massive down payment to get started. Here's a quick breakdown of what's actually possible:

• **FHA Loan** — as low as 3.5% down (with a 580+ credit score)
• **Conventional Loan** — as low as 3% down for qualifying buyers
• **USDA Loan** — 0% down for eligible properties in Florida's rural areas
• **VA Loan** — 0% down for eligible veterans and active-duty service members

And if you're a Florida first-time buyer or frontline worker, check out the **Florida Hometown Heroes** program — it offers down payment and closing cost assistance to help bridge the gap.

Every situation is different, so the best step is to see which loan type fits your income, credit, and goals.
