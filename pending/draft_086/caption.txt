FHA or Conventional — which loan is right for you? 🏡

There's no one-size-fits-all answer in homebuying. Understanding the difference between these two loan types can save you thousands — and help you qualify faster.

✅ FHA Loans
• Down payments as low as 3.5% (580+ credit score)
• More flexible qualifying guidelines
• Great for buyers building or rebuilding credit
• Requires mortgage insurance premium (MIP)

✅ Conventional Loans
• Down payments starting at 3% for qualified buyers
• PMI can be removed once you hit 20% equity
• Generally requires a stronger credit profile (620+)
• No upfront mortgage insurance premium

The right loan depends on your credit score, savings, and long-term goals. As your Florida mortgage resource, I'm here to walk you through your real options — no pressure, just clarity.
