What is PMI — and do you actually have to pay it? 🏠

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is an extra monthly charge added to most conventional loans when your down payment is less than 20%. Here's the important part: PMI protects the lender — not you.

Here's what first-time buyers often don't realize:
• PMI typically adds 0.5%–1.5% of your loan amount per year to your payments
• It's not permanent — once you hit 20% equity, you can request cancellation
• Some loan programs let you skip PMI entirely from day one

Ways to avoid PMI altogether:
✅ VA Loans — no PMI, ever, for eligible veterans and service members
✅ USDA Loans — no traditional PMI (a guarantee fee applies, often lower)
✅ Conventional loan with 20%+ down

Knowing your options before you apply can save you thousands over the life of your loan. Understanding the full picture is what separates a stressful homebuying experience from a confident one.
