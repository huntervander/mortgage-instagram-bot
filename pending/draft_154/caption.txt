FHA vs. Conventional — Which loan is right for you? 🏠

One of the most common questions first-time homebuyers ask is: "Should I go FHA or Conventional?" Here's a quick breakdown so you can walk in informed:

🏦 FHA Loans
• Minimum 3.5% down (with 580+ credit score)
• More flexible credit requirements
• Mortgage insurance (MIP) required for the life of the loan
• Great for buyers building credit or with limited savings

🏡 Conventional Loans
• As low as 3% down available
• Typically requires 620+ credit score
• PMI can be removed once you reach 20% equity
• Often lower long-term cost for buyers with strong credit

Neither is universally "better" — it depends on your credit score, savings, and long-term homeownership goals. The right loan is the one that fits YOUR situation.
