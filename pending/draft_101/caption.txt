Did you know your credit score could be the difference between thousands of dollars saved — or spent — on your mortgage? 🏠

Your credit score is one of the biggest factors lenders look at when determining your interest rate. Here's a quick breakdown of how it works:

📊 Score ranges and what they mean:
• 760+ → Best available rates (you're in great shape!)
• 720–759 → Still excellent — minimal rate impact
• 680–719 → Good, but you may pay slightly more
• 640–679 → You can often still qualify, but rates climb
• Below 640 → Harder to qualify; FHA loans may be an option

💡 Quick tips to boost your score before you buy:
• Pay down credit card balances below 30% of your limit
• Don't open new accounts or close old ones
• Set up auto-pay so you never miss a due date
• Check your credit report for errors (free at AnnualCreditReport.com)

Even moving your score up 20–40 points before applying can save you real money over the life of your loan.
