You DON'T need 20% down to buy a home in Florida. 🏠

This is one of the biggest myths holding buyers back — and it's keeping way too many families on the sidelines longer than they need to be.

Here's a quick look at your real options:

• **Conventional** — as low as 3% down for qualified buyers
• **FHA** — as low as 3.5% down (more flexible on credit scores)
• **VA** — 0% down for eligible veterans & active-duty service members (no PMI either!)
• **USDA** — 0% down in qualifying Florida rural areas

And if you're buying in Florida, the **Hometown Heroes program** may even help with down payment and closing cost assistance for eligible full-time workers — nurses, teachers, firefighters, law enforcement, and more.
