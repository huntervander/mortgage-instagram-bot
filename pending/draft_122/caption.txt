Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again! 🏡

One of the biggest myths holding Floridians back from homeownership is the "20% down payment" rule. The truth? You have far more options — and you might be closer to the keys than you realize.

Here's a quick look at low and zero-down loan programs available to Florida buyers:

• FHA Loan – as little as 3.5% down (with qualifying credit score)
• Conventional Loan – some programs allow just 3% down
• USDA Loan – 0% down for eligible rural areas in Florida
• VA Loan – 0% down for eligible veterans & active-duty service members
• Florida Hometown Heroes Program – down payment assistance for Florida's workforce
