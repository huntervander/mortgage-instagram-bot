🏡 VA Loan Myths — Busted.

If you served our country, you may be sitting on one of the most powerful home loan benefits available — and not even know it. Let's clear up a few common misconceptions.

❌ Myth #1: "I need 20% down."
✅ Truth: Eligible VA loan borrowers can purchase a home with $0 down payment. Zero.

❌ Myth #2: "VA loans are harder to get approved."
✅ Truth: VA loans often have more flexible credit requirements than conventional loans, and no private mortgage insurance (PMI) is required — saving you money every single month.

❌ Myth #3: "I can only use my VA benefit once."
✅ Truth: Your VA loan entitlement can be restored and used multiple times throughout your life. Own a home already? You may still be able to use it.
