📊 What Credit Score Do You Need to Buy a Home?

If you're thinking about buying a home in Florida, your credit score is one of the most important numbers a lender will look at — and knowing the basics can save you time and money.

Here's a quick breakdown by loan type:
• Conventional loans — typically 620+ minimum
• FHA loans — may qualify with 580+ (3.5% down) or 500–579 (10% down)
• VA loans — no official minimum, but most lenders look for 580–620+
• USDA loans — typically 640+

Even if your score isn't where you'd like it to be, small steps make a real difference:
✅ Pay every bill on time — payment history is 35% of your score
✅ Keep credit card balances below 30% of your credit limit
✅ Avoid opening new credit accounts right before applying for a mortgage
