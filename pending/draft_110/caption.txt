💳 Your Credit Score Can Make or Break Your Mortgage Rate

Before you start house hunting, this is the number you need to know — your credit score directly affects what interest rate you qualify for, and even a small difference can save (or cost) you thousands of dollars over the life of your loan.

Here's a quick breakdown of how lenders typically view scores:
• 800–850 | Exceptional — Best available rates
• 740–799 | Very Good — Strong approval odds & competitive rates
• 670–739 | Good — Qualifies for most conventional loans
• 580–669 | Fair — FHA loans are often a great fit here
• Below 580 | Poor — Focus on rebuilding credit before applying

Simple moves that can boost your score:
✅ Keep credit card balances below 30% of your credit limit
✅ Never miss a payment — set up autopay if you need to
✅ Hold off on opening new credit accounts before you apply for a mortgage
