Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again. 🏠

FHA loans are one of the most popular paths to homeownership for first-time buyers — and here's why:

✅ Down payment as low as 3.5% (with a 580+ credit score)
✅ More flexible credit requirements than conventional loans
✅ Competitive interest rates for buyers with moderate credit
✅ Sellers can contribute up to 6% toward your closing costs

The trade-off: FHA loans do require mortgage insurance (upfront + monthly). For many buyers, that's still a worthwhile step toward building equity and owning your home.

💡 Florida tip: Pair an FHA loan with the Florida Hometown Heroes program and you may qualify for down payment and closing cost assistance — making that first purchase even more within reach.
