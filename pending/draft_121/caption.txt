FHA vs. Conventional — which loan is right for you? 🏠

If you're house-hunting in Florida, you've probably heard both terms. Here's a quick breakdown so you know what you're working with:

🔵 FHA Loan
• As low as 3.5% down (with a 580+ credit score)
• Easier to qualify — great for first-time buyers or anyone rebuilding credit
• Requires mortgage insurance (MIP) — in most cases, for the life of the loan

⚪ Conventional Loan
• Down payments from 3–20% (higher down = better rate)
• Typically requires a 620+ credit score
• PMI can be removed once you hit 20% equity — unlike FHA MIP

Neither is "better" than the other — the right choice depends on your credit score, how much you have saved, and your long-term goals. That's exactly what we help you figure out!
