🏡 Think you need 20% down to buy a home in Florida? Think again.

Many Florida buyers are surprised to learn that down payment assistance programs can dramatically reduce — or even eliminate — what you need upfront. Here are a few worth knowing about:

✅ **Florida Hometown Heroes** — Designed for community workers (teachers, nurses, firefighters, law enforcement, and more). Offers up to 5% of the loan amount in down payment and closing cost assistance.

✅ **Florida Housing First-Time Buyer Programs** — Offers below-market interest rates paired with down payment help for eligible first-time buyers statewide.

✅ **FHA Loans** — Require as little as 3.5% down with a qualifying credit score. A great entry point for many buyers.

✅ **USDA Loans** — Zero down payment option for eligible buyers in qualifying rural and suburban areas of Florida.

The key is knowing which programs you qualify for — and that's exactly what we help you figure out.
