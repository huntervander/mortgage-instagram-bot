FHA vs. Conventional — which home loan is right for you? 🏡

Two of the most popular mortgage options, but they're not the same! Here's a quick breakdown to help you decide:

📋 FHA Loan
• Minimum 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score
• More flexible credit requirements
• Mortgage insurance (MIP) required for most borrowers

📋 Conventional Loan
• As low as 3% down for qualifying buyers
• Generally stronger credit needed (typically 620+)
• Private mortgage insurance (PMI) drops off once you hit 20% equity

Which one wins? It depends on YOUR situation — your credit score, savings, and long-term goals all factor in. There's no one-size-fits-all answer, and that's exactly why it helps to talk through your options with a licensed loan officer.
