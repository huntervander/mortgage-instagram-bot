Pre-Qualified vs. Pre-Approved — Do you know the difference? 🏠

If you're starting your homebuying journey in Florida, knowing the difference can save you time and stress.

✅ Pre-Qualification is a quick estimate of what you might be able to borrow, based on basic info you provide. No credit check required.

✅ Pre-Approval is a deeper review — your lender checks your credit, verifies income, assets, and more. It carries real weight with sellers.

💡 In today's competitive Florida market, sellers often prefer buyers with a solid pre-approval letter. It shows you're serious and financially ready.
