FHA vs. Conventional — which home loan is right for you? 🏡

Not all mortgages are created equal. Here's what you need to know:

🔵 FHA Loans
✅ Credit scores as low as 580 may qualify
✅ Down payment as low as 3.5%
✅ Great option for first-time homebuyers
⚠️ Requires mortgage insurance (MIP) — typically for_the life of the loan

🟢 Conventional Loans
✅ Typically requires a 620+ credit score
✅ Down payment options from 3–20%
✅ Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) can be removed once you reach 20% equity
⚠️ Stricter qualifying guidelines, but more flexibility long-term

The right loan depends on YOUR credit score, savings, and long-term goals. There's no one-size-fits-all answer — that's why having the right mortgage professional in your corner matters.
